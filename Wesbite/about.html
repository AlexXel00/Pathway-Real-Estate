Our Story

We Fell in Love
with Palawan

Almost two years ago, we made a decision that changed everything. We visited Palawan and simply could not bring ourselves to leave. The landscapes, the pace, the raw beauty of El Nido, Coron, and Port Barton. Something here felt like it was meant to be.

We decided to invest in this place, to make it our home, and to build something meaningful. Pathway Real Estate was born from that decision: a professional platform built by people who genuinely live here and believe in what Palawan is becoming.

Today, we work alongside some of the most experienced local professionals in the region. People who know the land, the regulations, the communities. That combination of international perspective and deep local knowledge is what sets Pathway apart.
