Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal data.
Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Pathway Real Estate, the real estate brand of Pathway Partners, a corporation registered with the Philippine Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC Reg. No. 2026030242643-10), with its office at A.P. Padrones 1 St., San Pedro, 5300 Puerto Princesa City, Palawan, Philippines.

We are the personal information controller for the data described in this policy. For any question about your data, contact us at info@pathwayphilippines.com. Our Data Protection Officer can be reached at the same address.

2. What data we collect

When you use our contact form, we receive the information you enter: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, the type of inquiry and property, your preferred location, your budget or asking price, whether you are a foreign buyer, and your message.

When you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we receive your contact details and the content of your messages.

When you visit the website, our hosting provider automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you open and the time of your visit. This is needed to deliver the website securely.

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies on this website.

3. Why we use your data

  • To answer your inquiry and send you information about properties and services you asked for
  • To prepare and carry out a purchase, sale, lease or construction project with you
  • To meet our legal obligations, for example for tax and transaction records
  • To operate and protect this website

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for purposes you would not reasonably expect.

4. Legal basis

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), we process your data based on your consent when you contact us, because it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into an agreement or to fulfil one, and where required to comply with legal obligations.

If you are located in the European Union, the same purposes are covered by Article 6(1)(a), (b), (c) and (f) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): your consent, steps before or performance of a contract, legal obligations and our legitimate interest in running a secure website and answering inquiries.

5. Who we share your data with

We only share your data where it is needed for the purposes above:

  • Service providers who help us run our business: our website host (Netlify) and form handling, our email provider (Google Workspace) and WhatsApp (Meta) if you contact us there
  • Partners involved in your transaction, such as lawyers, licensed brokers, property owners, developers, architects or contractors, but only when this is needed for your request and with your knowledge
  • Authorities, where the law requires it

Some of these providers are based outside the Philippines, for example in the United States. Where data is transferred abroad, we rely on the providers' contractual and security commitments to protect it.

6. Third-party content on this website

Some parts of the website load content from other providers. When this happens, your browser connects to their servers and they may receive your IP address:

  • Google Fonts, for the typefaces of this website
  • Google Maps, for the location maps in our property and condo details
  • Embedded videos and posts on our News page (for example YouTube, Vimeo, Instagram, Facebook or TikTok), which may set their own cookies

The data protection policies of these providers apply to their services.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep inquiry data for as long as needed to handle your request and any business relationship that follows. If no transaction results, we delete or anonymise your inquiry within 24 months of our last contact. Data connected to a completed transaction is kept for as long as Philippine law requires.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to object to its processing, to have it corrected, to have it erased or blocked, to data portability, and to claim damages. You can also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines.

If the GDPR applies to you, you additionally have the right to restrict processing, to withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, and to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

To use any of these rights, simply email info@pathwayphilippines.com.

9. Security

We protect your data with appropriate organisational and technical measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access only for team members who need it, and reputable service providers.

10. Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from persons under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The current version is always available on this page.
