Whether you are buying or selling property in Palawan, the process should feel clear, professional and well guided. At Pathway Real Estate, we offer a complete suite of services, from finding the right land to handing over the keys to your finished home, and from presenting your property to qualified buyers to supporting the sales process with trusted local guidance.

The beginning

Property Search & Sourcing

Whether you are looking for a beachfront lot, a condo with ocean views, a house with land or a remote agricultural plot: we know Palawan. We work with a network of trusted sellers across El Nido, Port Barton, San Vicente and Coron to find you the right property at the right price.

We listen carefully to your vision, your budget and your timeline. Then we do the searching so you don't have to. Every property we present has been personally reviewed and meets our standards for location, title clarity and value.

The purchase

Contract & Closing

Purchasing property in the Philippines as a foreigner comes with specific legal requirements. We guide you through every step, from the initial offer and due diligence to the signing of the Deed of Sale and the transfer of title.

We work with licensed Philippine attorneys and brokers to ensure your purchase is fully compliant with Philippine law. You will always know exactly where you stand and what comes next.

03
The paperwork

Building Permits

Navigating the permit process in the Philippines can be complex and time-consuming. We handle it for you. From the Building Permit and Electrical Permit to the Sanitary Permit and Occupancy Certificate, we manage the entire application process with the local government units in Palawan.

Our team has established relationships with the relevant authorities in El Nido, San Vicente and Puerto Princesa, which means fewer delays and less frustration for you.

Building PermitElectrical PermitSanitary PermitOccupancy CertificateLGU Liaison

The vision

Architectural Planning

You have a piece of land. Now what? We work with experienced local architects to translate your vision into professional plans. Whether you want a simple beachside bungalow or a full villa with multiple structures, we coordinate the design process from concept to construction-ready drawings.

All designs are created with the local climate, terrain and building regulations in mind. We also ensure that your architectural plans meet the requirements for permit applications.
