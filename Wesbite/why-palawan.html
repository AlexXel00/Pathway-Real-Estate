Investment Case

Why invest here?

Six compelling reasons Palawan is one of Southeast Asia's most exciting real estate markets.

01

Still affordable

Compared to Bali, Phuket or the Maldives, Palawan land prices remain significantly lower, with far greater upside potential as tourism and infrastructure continue to grow.

02

Tourism is booming

Palawan welcomed 2.13 million visitors in 2025, who spent an estimated PHP 65.5 billion, and nearly half came from abroad. El Nido and Coron lead the way; San Vicente and Port Barton are just beginning their rise.

03

Rental potential

El Nido's short-term rental market is active and growing: nearly 1,400 listings earn an average of about USD 12,000 a year, and nightly rates rose 9% in the past year to around USD 108 (AirDNA, July 2026). These are averages across all listings, including budget rooms. Well-located, well-managed villas aim higher, and we walk you through realistic numbers for each property.

04

Infrastructure investment

New roads, expanded airports and government-backed tourism development are rapidly improving connectivity across the island and driving property values upward.

05

Foreigner-friendly

While foreigners cannot own land outright in the Philippines, condo ownership and long-term leases are well-established legal routes. Our lease structure runs for 50 years, in two 25-year terms, documented by a Philippine lawyer.

06

Protected nature

Palawan is a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve. Strict environmental protections mean the island's natural beauty, its core asset, is safeguarded for generations to come.

The Locations

Where we work

We are active across the entire island of Palawan, from the northern tip of Coron all the way south to the shores of Balabac. Wherever the right property is, we will find it for you.

Our team has deep local knowledge of every major location on the island. Below you will find the five areas where we focus most of our work, each with its own character, pace and opportunity.

El Nido Coron Port Barton San Vicente Puerto Princesa

The icon

El Nido

El Nido is Palawan's most celebrated destination, famous worldwide for its towering limestone karsts, hidden lagoons and crystal-clear waters. It is the most established market, with strong tourist numbers and a growing international community. Land here is premium, but the demand is consistent and the brand recognition is unmatched.

Limestone Karsts Island Hopping Premium Market International Community

The hidden gem

Port Barton

Port Barton is what El Nido was fifteen years ago: unhurried, unspoiled and just beginning to attract attention. The white-sand beaches, calm bays and relaxed atmosphere make it ideal for boutique resort development or a personal retreat. Property prices remain accessible, and the growth trajectory is clear.

Affordable Entry Boutique Potential White Sand Beaches Early Market

The rising star

San Vicente & Long Beach

San Vicente is home to Long Beach. At 14.7 kilometres, it is the longest white-sand beach in the Philippines and one of the longest in Asia. The town has its own airport, opened in 2018, and investment in roads and tourism infrastructure continues. This is where some of the most exciting opportunities in Palawan currently exist.

Longest Beach in PH Own Airport Up & Coming High Growth Potential

The diver's paradise

Coron

Coron sits in the northern Calamian Islands and is renowned for some of the world's best wreck diving, pristine lakes and dramatic scenery. It attracts a distinct traveller: adventurous, nature-conscious and willing to pay for quality. The real estate market here is growing steadily, with strong interest from both local and foreign investors.

World-Class Diving Scenic Lakes Steady Growth Distinct Market

The gateway

Puerto Princesa

Puerto Princesa is the capital of Palawan and the main gateway to the island. Home to the famous Underground River, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, it is the most accessible and well-connected city in Palawan, with direct flights from Manila, Cebu and other Philippine cities. Its airport is classified as international and has handled direct flights from Taipei and Hong Kong in the past; new international routes, including a planned direct connection to South Korea, are being pursued. It offers a more urban lifestyle while still being close to pristine nature, and is an ideal base for those who want easy access to the rest of the island.

UNESCO World Heritage International Airport Urban Lifestyle Main Gateway

Market Overview

The market today

An honest look at where Palawan real estate stands, and where it is heading.

Land is in demand

As visitor numbers and investor interest grow, well-located land in Palawan's tourist hotspots is getting harder to find. Beachfront land in El Nido commands premium prices, and Port Barton and San Vicente are following as they gain international exposure. Entry-level lots in less-developed areas still start from PHP 2 to 3 million for well-located agricultural land.

Demand exceeds supply

Quality accommodation in Palawan is in high demand during peak season, while well-designed, well-managed properties remain scarce. That means buyers who build now are entering a market where rental demand is already proven and growing.

Title status matters

Not all land in Palawan carries a full Torrens Title. A&D (Alienable and Disposable) land and Tax Declaration properties are common and can be converted, but require careful due diligence. We guide every buyer through this process to ensure full legal clarity before any purchase is made.
