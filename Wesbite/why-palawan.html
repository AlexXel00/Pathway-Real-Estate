Investment Case

Why invest here?

Six compelling reasons Palawan is one of Southeast Asia's most exciting real estate markets.

01

Still affordable

Compared to Bali, Phuket or the Maldives, Palawan land prices remain significantly lower, with far greater upside potential as tourism and infrastructure continue to grow.

02

Tourism is booming

Palawan welcomed 2.13 million visitors in 2025, who spent an estimated PHP 65.5 billion, and nearly half came from abroad. El Nido and Coron lead the way; San Vicente and Port Barton are just beginning their rise.

03

Rental potential

El Nido's short-term rental market is active and growing: nearly 1,400 listings earn an average of about USD 12,000 a year, and nightly rates rose 9% in the past year to around USD 108 (AirDNA, July 2026). These are averages across all listings, including budget rooms. Well-located, well-managed villas aim higher, and we walk you through realistic numbers for each property.

04

Infrastructure investment

New roads, expanded airports and government-backed tourism development are rapidly improving connectivity across the island and driving property values upward.

05

Foreigner-friendly

While foreigners cannot own land outright in the Philippines, condo ownership and long-term leases are well-established legal routes. Our lease structure runs for 50 years, in two 25-year terms, documented by a Philippine lawyer.

06

Protected nature

Palawan is a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve. Strict environmental protections mean the island's natural beauty, its core asset, is safeguarded for generations to come.

Market Overview

The market today

An honest look at where Palawan real estate stands, and where it is heading.

Land is in demand

As visitor numbers and investor interest grow, well-located land in Palawan's tourist hotspots is getting harder to find. Beachfront land in El Nido commands premium prices, and Port Barton and San Vicente are following as they gain international exposure. Entry-level lots in less-developed areas still start from PHP 2 to 3 million for well-located agricultural land.

Demand exceeds supply

Quality accommodation in Palawan is in high demand during peak season, while well-designed, well-managed properties remain scarce. That means buyers who build now are entering a market where rental demand is already proven and growing.

Title status matters

Not all land in Palawan carries a full Torrens Title. A&D (Alienable and Disposable) land and Tax Declaration properties are common and can be converted, but require careful due diligence. We guide every buyer through this process to ensure full legal clarity before any purchase is made.

The life

More than an investment

For many of our clients, Palawan is not just a financial decision. It is a life decision.

Palawan offers something increasingly rare in today's world: space, silence and natural beauty on a scale that is almost impossible to find elsewhere. Whether you are looking for a second home, a retirement destination or a base for a new chapter, life here moves at a pace that reminds you what matters.

  • Year-round tropical climate with average temperatures of 27 to 32°C
  • World-class diving, snorkelling, island hopping and water sports
  • Growing expat community with European, American and Australian residents
  • Low cost of living compared to Europe, Singapore or Australia
  • English widely spoken, making it one of the easiest countries in Asia to settle in
  • Direct flights from Manila, and growing international connectivity
  • Friendly, welcoming local culture with strong community spirit
